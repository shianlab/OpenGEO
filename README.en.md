## ✨ What is this?

**OpenGEO** is an open knowledge base dedicated to **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)**.

As more users stop clicking through search result pages and instead ask generative engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Kimi, DeepSeek, Doubao and others — directly for answers, **"being cited by AI" is replacing "ranking in search" as the new gateway to traffic and trust.**

OpenGEO systematically organizes the theory, mechanisms, concepts, methods, tools, cases, and industry developments of GEO, and continuously curates academic papers and industry reports from around the world. Our goals:

- 📐 Build an **open, reusable, and verifiable** body of GEO knowledge;
- 🔬 Ground every conclusion in three evidence chains: **academic research, industry reports, and hands-on practice**;
- 🌏 Provide a **free, freely citable** public knowledge foundation for creators, brands, and researchers — in Chinese and globally.

> ### 💡 In one sentence
>
> **SEO gets people to find you. GEO gets AI to recommend you.**

## 🧭 What is GEO?

**GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** is the practice of optimizing content and sources for AI answer engines: through structured content, authoritative source building, and semantic positioning, it makes brands, products, and viewpoints more likely to be cited accurately and prominently in generative answers.

| Dimension | Traditional SEO | GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gateway** | Search engine results page (a list of blue links) | Conversational AI answers (directly generated responses) |
| **User behavior** | Type keywords → click and compare results one by one | Ask a question → trust the answer synthesized by AI |
| **Slots available** | ~10 organic results per page | Typically only **2–5 sources** cited per answer — slots are extremely scarce |
| **Optimized for** | Keywords, backlinks, page structure, authority | Semantic structure, citable snippets, authoritative sources, entity recognition |
| **Outcome** | Clicks and visits | Being **written into the answer** — quoted and recommended directly |
| **Measurement** | Rankings, CTR, organic traffic | AI citation rate, mention accuracy, source share of voice |

GEO does not replace SEO; it is the essential next layer under a new distribution logic where **the answer itself is the entry point**.

Every curated source receives a **unified catalogue entry**: author/institution, publication year, language and region, topic tags, evidence grade, an objective one-sentence summary, and a link to the public original.

## 🔄 How Content Is Updated

OpenGEO follows an **"maintain once, publish everywhere"** pipeline:

1. **Collect & catalogue** — new content is graded, tagged, summarized in one sentence, and linked to its original source in the online knowledge base;
2. **Review & version** — entries go through a unified catalogue for review, numbering, and version control, keeping every record traceable;
3. **Publish openly** — reviewed content is synced to this GitHub repository and the project website; GitHub serves as the public mirror, version history, and community collaboration layer;
4. **Iterate continuously** — updates increment version numbers; retractions and corrections are also logged, so citers always get a trustworthy version.

> The Feishu online knowledge base is the richest and fastest-updated reading experience; GitHub is best for tracking changes, citing source files, and contributing.

## 🤝 Contributing

OpenGEO is built in the open. Contributions via **Issues** and **Pull Requests** are welcome:

- **Suggest sources** — papers, reports, or cases worth curating (please include public source links);
- **Expand entries** — improve glossary and mechanism explanations;
- **Share cases** — contribute your own GEO practice and data observations;
- **Correct errors** — report dead links, factual mistakes, or mislabeled content.

Inclusion criteria: **publicly traceable, clearly graded, objective and neutral.** All external sources must retain their original attribution; commercially positioned materials must be labeled as such.
